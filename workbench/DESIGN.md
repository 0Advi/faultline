# Workbench design

Visual thesis: a bright, quiet operator workspace for an engineering team reviewing
experiments together on laptops in a meeting room, with ink text and muted teal actions.
Content plan: navigation, planning workspace, readiness inspector, run timeline,
evidence/history, then exact patch review and replay comparison.
Interaction thesis: short panel entrance, clear focus/selection feedback, and stable
progress updates that respect reduced motion. No decorative animation during incidents.
Fixtures are visibly labeled on every screen. Cleanup and recovery are separate
from patch deployment. The interface never infers readiness from a button click.
